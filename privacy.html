Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Who We Are

Ananta Home Pvt. Ltd. is a boutique hotel located at Z Street, Thamel, Kathmandu, Nepal 44600. We operate the website at hotelanantahome.com. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at anantahomenepal@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect your full name, email address, preferred check-in and check-out dates, room type preference, number of guests, and any message you choose to include. We do not collect payment card details through this website. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising networks.

Why We Collect It

We collect this information solely to respond to your booking enquiry and to arrange your stay at Ananta Home. We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent.

Who We Share It With

Your enquiry data is processed through EmailJS (emailjs.com), a third-party email delivery service, in order to deliver your message to our inbox. EmailJS processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your personal data with any other third parties.

Our website also embeds Google Maps to display our location. Google’s privacy policy applies to any data collected through that embedded map.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain enquiry emails for up to 24 months for the purpose of booking records and guest correspondence. If you would like your data deleted, please email us at anantahomenepal@gmail.com and we will action your request within 14 days.

Your Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions with data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Please contact us at anantahomenepal@gmail.com to exercise these rights.
