Welcome to Ananta Home

नमस्ते

Tucked away in a quiet corner of Thamel, our hotel offers an intimate stay defined by comfort, personalized service, and an unbeatable location. Every detail is centered around making guests feel welcomed and connected to the spirit of Kathmandu. Whether you are here to explore, relax, or work remotely, you will always find a warm and peaceful place to return to.

Ananta, in Sanskrit, means infinite. It is infinity of care that guides everything here — in the details of each room, in the unhurried quality of each morning, in staff who inhabit your stay alongside you rather than managing it from a distance.
